## 3. What synthetic data can and cannot substitute for

**Can substitute (offline, today):**
- Task-definition statistics: length, NPC design, grader taxonomy, service surface, harm keywords.
- Contrastive trajectory pairs for critic / safety-monitor training (150 graded pairs).
- Grader-quality audits: synthetic set has 0% empty graders vs og 15.6%.
- Mechanism-taxonomy coverage mapping (v5 seeds → outcome×mechanism cells).

**Cannot substitute (blocked):**
- Real model unsafe rates on synthetic tasks — no completed passive runs exist.
- LLM-judge labels on synthetic rollouts — none were produced.
- Like-for-like og-vs-synthetic trajectory unsafe comparison — synthetic side is constructed, not rolled out.
- Re-scoring og tasks whose graders call an LLM inside the grader (proxy-blocked).

## 4. Blocked on LiteLLM proxy
